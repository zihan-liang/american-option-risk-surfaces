\subsection{Method}
Use time-to-maturity $\tau=T-t$ and normalize experiments to $K=1$ unless otherwise stated.

\subsection{Mathematical Formulation}
\[
dS_t=(r-q)S_tdt+\sigma S_tdW_t,
\quad
\mathcal{L}U=\frac{1}{2}\sigma^2S^2U_{SS}+(r-q)SU_S-rU.
\]
\[
U\ge \Phi,\quad U_\tau-\mathcal{L}U\ge0,\quad
(U-\Phi)(U_\tau-\mathcal{L}U)=0.
\]
The continuation premium is $C=U-\Phi\ge0$.
