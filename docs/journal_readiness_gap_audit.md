# Journal Readiness Gap Audit

This audit records what must change for the project to read like a journal-style manuscript rather than a stage-by-stage SURF report. It does not upgrade the scientific claims by wording alone.

## Main-Text Ready Claims

- The CN/PSOR benchmark is suitable as an engineering-scale validated label generator within the tested Black--Scholes American put and dividend-call regimes.
- The positive continuation-premium surrogate hard-enforces the payoff obstacle and is suitable for a scoped price/premium acceleration claim.
- The bounded supervised Delta head is suitable for a strict-mask Delta diagnostic claim.

## Claims Kept Limited Or Supplementary

- Boundary-head results remain weaker than price and Delta results, especially for dividend-call refinement; they should be shown as diagnostic evidence and limitation, not as an exact free-boundary solver.
- The integrated workflow has only six fresh PSOR audit cases in the current evidence package. It supports scenario-screening language only and should not be a broad top-journal primary claim.
- Gamma is a finite-difference diagnostic only; no Gamma surrogate claim is supported.

## Reviewer-Facing Requirements Added In Stage 15

- A manuscript and supplementary material split.
- A publication gap matrix mapping claims to evidence and manuscript placement.
- Publication figures with a manifest linking each figure to source artifacts.
- Claim-language tests that reject positive overclaims while allowing restricted negative wording.
