# Future Extensions

Future work must remain separate from the current claims.

1. Larger regime envelope: more maturities, volatilities, rates, dividends, and stress cases.
2. Active learning: use surrogate error or disagreement to decide where PSOR labels are most valuable.
3. Shape-constrained models: prefer architectural guarantees over weak penalty-only constraints.
4. Uncertainty quantification: ensembles or conformal intervals for price, boundary, and Delta heads.
5. Alternative dynamics: local volatility, stochastic volatility, jumps, or rough volatility, each requiring new validation.
