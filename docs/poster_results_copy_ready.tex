% ---------- B. Matched American-Put Price Slices ----------
\textbf{B. Matched American-Put Price Slices}

\noindent
Matched contract: $K=1$, $T=1$, $r=0.05$, $q=0.10$, $\sigma=0.60$; seed 101. The high-accuracy DIRK reference and converged binomial tree provide classical checks. The existing three-maturity price-slice figure may be retained unchanged.

% ---------- C. Signed Price Error ----------
\textbf{C. Signed Price Error}

\noindent
On the common domain $S/K\in[0.5,1.5]$, $\tau/T\in[0.01,1]$, Arm D reduced shared-domain DIRK price RMSE by \textbf{96.3\%} relative to Arm C ($0.00105$ vs. $0.02788$). At the predeclared middle dimension $d=20$, FC--HVI and DeepSets--HVI gave RMSE $0.00251$ and $0.00332$, respectively.

% ---------- D. Free Boundary ----------
\textbf{D. Free Boundary}

\noindent
Arm D recovered substantially more of the matched low-dimensional stopping boundary than Arm C. In the final high-dimensional runs, however, FC--HVI and DeepSets--HVI at $d=10,20,50$ all had \textbf{0\% primary free-boundary coverage} at threshold $10^{-6}$ (0 predicted points on a 201-point reference grid). No synthetic HD boundary curve should be drawn.

% ---------- E. High-Dimensional Architecture Diagnostics ----------
\textbf{E. High-Dimensional Architecture Diagnostics}

\noindent
DeepSets used \textbf{26--30\% fewer parameters} and achieved near-exact permutation consistency ($\approx2.3$--$2.6\times10^{-8}$ RMSE), whereas FC--HVI permutation inconsistency was $\approx4.5\times10^{-4}$ to $1.1\times10^{-3}$. FC--HVI nevertheless achieved lower shared-domain DIRK price RMSE at all tested dimensions, and DeepSets showed no runtime advantage in this implementation.

\noindent\footnotesize
The HD comparison is post-hoc matched-contract, single-seed (101), diagnostic-only. Low-dimensional Arm C/D are physics-only held-out PINNs, whereas the HD runs are oracle-assisted supervised+HVI; cross-protocol runtime ratios are therefore not reported as speedups.
\normalsize
